# Trinethra Context

## Fellow Model

DT Fellows work inside manufacturing companies to improve execution and build operational systems.

There are two layers of work:

### Layer 1 — Execution
- Daily follow-ups
- Coordination
- Operational tracking
- Task execution

### Layer 2 — Systems Building
- SOP creation
- Dashboards
- Accountability systems
- Sustainable workflows

The key evaluation question:

"If the Fellow leaves tomorrow, does the system continue running?"

## Critical Rubric Boundary

### Score 6
Reliable executor of assigned tasks.

### Score 7
Independently identifies problems and expands scope.

This distinction is heavily emphasized in analysis.

## Supervisor Biases

The system attempts to account for:
- Helpfulness bias
- Presence bias
- Halo/horn effect
- Recency bias

## AI Design Goal

The tool is AI-assisted, not AI-automated.

Human reviewers remain final decision-makers.
